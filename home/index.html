What is the IKEA SKÅDIS?

The IKEA SKÅDIS is a popular modular pegboard system. Using attachments that slot into the holes, you can mount all sorts of things on the pegboard without the need for screws or adhesives. Because of how versatile it is, making attachments for the SKÅDIS is often considered an amazing 3D modelling exercise.

What is this YSWS?

The premise is simple! Ship a 3D design that interfaces with the IKEA SKÅDIS, and we'll ship you a SKÅDIS! The size of the pegboard will depend on how many hours you've spent:
  • Small (36x56cm) - 3 hours
  • Medium (56x56cm) - 5.5 hours
  • Large (76x56cm) - 8 hours
Exceptional projects may also receive a BLÅHAJ :D

Submission requirements:

  1. Your submission must be unique.
    *If a similar 3D model already exists, you will be asked to clarify what makes yours better.
  2. Your submission must have a purpose and solve a problem.
  3. You must track at least 3 hours on OnShape using Hackatime.
  4. You must be between the ages of 13 and 18 (inclusive).

What can I make?

Some ideas include a universal hook, battery holder, filament spool holder, headphone holder or a controller stand. You could even go crazy and design attachments that turn the SKÅDIS into a carriable accessory!
